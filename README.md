One line summing up the week — at most 100 characters, written by `main.py` to
`/output/entry.txt`:

> This week my phone died, my laptop stayed imaginary, and my best joke earned exactly two dollars.

The line is drawn from the entrant's real week: the main phone died, the week
ran on a borrowed phone, twelve make.fun one-liner entries went in, and the
only one that paid was a joke — exactly two dollars. Ten candidate lines were
written and ranked by hand against the kit's judge brief (see `candidates.md`);
this one won on the sharpest turn of phrase ("my laptop stayed imaginary") and
the most concrete detail no other week could claim.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (97 characters, no trailing
newline) and verifies it against the gate itself: UTF-8, 1–100 characters,
a single non-blank line, read back byte-for-byte after writing.
